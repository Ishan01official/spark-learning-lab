# Medallion Architecture

## Purpose

Explain Bronze, Silver, and Gold as progressive refinement, not arbitrary folders.

## Final image-generation prompt

```text
Generate one standalone 16:9 horizontal hand-drawn technical explainer on clean white paper. Use
rough black pen lines, simple buckets, arrows, tables, and sparse red/blue handwritten annotations.

Concept: Medallion architecture. Draw three simple refining stations from left to right: Bronze is
a rough bin of raw rocks and messy records; Silver is a cleaning table where a serious tiny black
helper character removes duplicates and fixes types; Gold is a small polished shelf with business
metric cards. Draw consumers on the far right as simple glasses or report pages.

The core idea: Bronze preserves raw data, Silver makes it trustworthy, Gold makes it business-ready.
```

## Layout description

Three stations left-to-right: Bronze raw bin, Silver cleaning table, Gold serving shelf, consumers
on far right. Arrows are simple and sparse.

## Labels to include

- `Bronze: raw`
- `Silver: clean`
- `Gold: serving`
- `dedupe`
- `quality checks`
- `business metrics` in blue

## Visual style

Notebook refinery metaphor, white background, black pen, small blue notes, one red check/fail mark
near quality checks, clean spacing.

## What to avoid

- Metallic 3D medals
- Corporate pipeline poster
- Too many layer subtypes
- Big paragraphs inside boxes

## Suggested caption

Bronze keeps the raw truth, Silver makes it trustworthy, and Gold turns it into business-ready
tables.
